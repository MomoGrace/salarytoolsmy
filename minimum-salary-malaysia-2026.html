Minimum Salary Malaysia 2026: Latest Updates and Rules

Understand the minimum monthly wage reference for Malaysian employees and what it means for take-home pay.

The minimum wage reference for Malaysia in 2026 is RM1,700 per month. This follows the move to RM1,700 under the Minimum Wages Order 2024, with wider alignment across employers from 2025.

This page is for general education and payroll planning only. Always refer to the latest official government notice or professional payroll advice for compliance decisions.

Does RM1,700 mean take-home pay?

No. RM1,700 refers to gross basic wage reference, not necessarily the final bank-in amount. The employee may still have EPF, SOCSO, EIS or other applicable payroll deductions.

Example: RM1,700 gross salary

If your gross salary is RM1,700, your take-home pay will usually be lower after employee deductions. The final amount depends on your payroll category and deductions.

FAQ

Is Malaysia minimum salary RM1,700 in 2026?

RM1,700 per month is the current minimum wage reference commonly used for 2026 planning.

Is minimum wage the same as net salary?

No. Minimum wage is a gross wage reference. Net salary is what remains after deductions.
