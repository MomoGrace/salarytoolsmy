Monthly Salary Deduction in Malaysia: EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB Guide

Monthly salary deductions in Malaysia usually include statutory contributions and tax deductions. The common items employees see are EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB, although the actual payroll slip may include other deductions too.

Common salary deductions

DeductionPurposeWho handles it?
EPFRetirement savings contributionEmployer payroll / KWSP
SOCSOSocial security protectionEmployer payroll / PERKESO
EISEmployment insurance contributionEmployer payroll / PERKESO
PCB / MTDMonthly income tax deductionEmployer payroll / LHDN

EPF deduction

EPF is usually the largest regular employee deduction. It reduces take-home pay today but contributes to retirement savings. The employee and employer shares are different, and only the employee share reduces gross salary.

SOCSO and EIS deductions

SOCSO and EIS are usually smaller than EPF, but they still matter for net salary. These deductions are based on PERKESO rules and schedules.

PCB deduction

PCB is the monthly tax deduction. It can be zero, small or significant depending on the employee profile. Higher salary levels generally make PCB more important, but the exact result depends on official calculation.

Other possible deductions

Some payroll slips may also show unpaid leave, advances, loans, insurance, benefits or company-specific deductions. Always check the payslip if the bank-in amount is lower than expected.

FAQ

What are the main salary deductions in Malaysia?

The common deductions are EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB.

Are all deductions the same every month?

Not always. Bonus, unpaid leave, relief updates, benefit changes and PCB calculation can change monthly net salary.

Which deduction is usually the largest?

For many employees, EPF is one of the largest regular deductions.

Where can I check official deduction rules?

Check KWSP for EPF, PERKESO for SOCSO/EIS and LHDN for PCB/MTD.
