EPF Calculation Example for RM3,000 Salary in Malaysia

If your monthly salary is RM3,000, your EPF contribution is usually estimated based on your wage and the contribution rate used for planning. This example helps you understand how EPF can affect take-home pay.

Quick example

Monthly salary: RM3,000

Estimated employee EPF at 11%: RM330

Estimated salary after employee EPF: RM2,670

Simple formula

RM3,000 × 11% = RM330

The employer contribution is separate and may be calculated differently from the employee contribution.
