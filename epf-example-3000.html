EPF Calculation Example for RM3,000 Salary in Malaysia

If your monthly salary is RM3,000, your EPF contribution is usually calculated based on your wage and the applicable employee and employer contribution rates.

Quick example

Monthly salary: RM3,000

Estimated employee EPF: RM330

Estimated salary after employee EPF: RM2,670

The simple calculation is RM3,000 × 11% = RM330. Employer contribution is separate and normally paid by the employer.
