How to Calculate Salary in Malaysia: Gross Pay, Deductions and Net Salary

Salary calculation in Malaysia starts with gross salary, then subtracts employee deductions such as EPF, SOCSO, EIS and possible PCB to estimate net salary. The final amount can vary because official schedules, rounding, tax reliefs and payroll settings matter.

Salary calculation formula in Malaysia

The simplest way to understand salary is: gross salary minus employee deductions equals estimated net salary. Gross salary is the amount stated before deductions. Net salary is the amount that is usually banked into the employee account after monthly payroll processing.

StepMeaning
Start with gross salaryUse the monthly salary stated in the offer letter or payroll record.
Subtract EPFEmployee retirement contribution, based on the applicable KWSP schedule.
Subtract SOCSO and EISEmployee social security and employment insurance deductions under PERKESO rules.
Subtract PCBMonthly tax deduction where applicable under LHDN calculation.
ResultEstimated take-home pay.

What counts as gross salary?

Gross salary usually refers to the amount before statutory deductions. It can include basic salary and, depending on the payroll situation, may also include allowances, commissions, overtime, bonuses or benefits. When comparing jobs, always ask whether the salary figure is gross or net.

Common salary deductions in Malaysia

The common deductions employees look at are EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB. EPF is mainly for retirement savings. SOCSO provides social security protection. EIS relates to employment insurance. PCB is a monthly income tax deduction that depends on LHDN payroll computation.

Why estimates are not always exact

Online salary pages can provide a useful planning estimate, but they cannot fully replace official payroll calculation. EPF and SOCSO can involve schedules and wage ceilings. PCB depends on tax profile, residence status, reliefs and employer payroll data.

Best way to compare job offers

When comparing two job offers, calculate the estimated take-home pay for each one. A higher gross salary is helpful, but net salary shows the monthly cash that can actually be used for rent, transport, loan payments, family support and savings.

FAQ

What is the basic salary calculation in Malaysia?

Start with gross monthly salary, then subtract employee EPF, SOCSO, EIS and possible PCB to estimate take-home pay.

Is gross salary the same as take-home salary?

No. Gross salary is before deductions, while take-home salary is after deductions.

Can two employees with the same salary have different take-home pay?

Yes. PCB, tax reliefs, age, residence status, benefits and payroll setup can create different results.

Should I use gross or net salary for budgeting?

Use net salary or estimated take-home pay for budgeting because it reflects the amount available after deductions.
