PCB Tax Malaysia Example: How Monthly Tax Deduction Works

PCB, also known as monthly tax deduction, is used in Malaysia payroll to deduct estimated income tax from an employee’s monthly salary.

Simple PCB example

Gross salary: Monthly salary before deductions

Common deductions: EPF, SOCSO, EIS

PCB: Estimated monthly tax deduction

Net salary: Salary after deductions

Your PCB amount may change if your salary changes, you receive a bonus, or your employer updates payroll data.
