What is take-home pay?

Take-home pay is the amount an employee receives after deductions. In Malaysia, common deductions include EPF, SOCSO, EIS and possible PCB tax deduction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this take-home pay calculator official?

No. It provides simplified estimates for planning only. Final payroll should follow official rules and employer payroll calculation.

Why is take-home pay lower than gross salary?

Because payroll deductions such as EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB may be deducted before salary is paid.
