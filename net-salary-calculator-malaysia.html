This calculator starts with your gross monthly salary and estimates common payroll deductions such as EPF, SOCSO, EIS and possible PCB. It is designed for employees, job seekers and small business planning in Malaysia.

Frequently asked questions

Is this salary calculator official?

No. It is a simplified estimator for planning only. Always refer to official sources or payroll professionals for final payroll calculations.

Why is take-home pay lower than gross salary?

Take-home pay is usually lower because EPF, SOCSO, EIS and possible PCB deductions may be taken from salary before payment.
