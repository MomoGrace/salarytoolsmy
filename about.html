About SalaryToolsMY

SalaryToolsMY provides free Malaysia salary and payroll estimation tools for employees, job seekers and small business planning.

What this website helps with

Our calculators are designed to help users estimate salary, take-home pay, EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB tax and related payroll figures in a simple way.

Who can use SalaryToolsMY

  • Employees who want to estimate take-home pay
  • Job seekers comparing salary offers
  • Small business owners doing rough payroll planning
  • Anyone who wants a quick Malaysia salary estimate before checking official figures

Important note

SalaryToolsMY is for general planning only. Calculator results are simplified estimates and should not be treated as official payroll, tax, legal or financial advice.
