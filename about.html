About

SalaryToolsMY provides free salary and payroll estimation tools for users in Malaysia.

SalaryToolsMY is a free online resource built to help Malaysian users quickly estimate salary, take-home pay, EPF, SOCSO, EIS, income tax, PCB, overtime and bonus-related figures.

Our goal is to make common payroll calculations easier to understand. The tools are simplified and should be used for general planning only.
