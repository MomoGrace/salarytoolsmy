PCB Deduction from Salary in Malaysia

Understand PCB deduction from salary in Malaysia and why monthly tax deduction can affect take-home pay.

EPFSOCSOEISPCB

Direct answer

PCB is the monthly tax deduction made through payroll. It can reduce take-home pay depending on salary, tax category, reliefs and payroll calculation.

Quick summary

TopicExplanation
What PCB meansPCB is commonly used to refer to monthly tax deduction. It helps spread income tax payment across the year through payroll.

What PCB means
