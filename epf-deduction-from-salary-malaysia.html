EPF Deduction from Salary in Malaysia: Employee Contribution Guide

EPF deduction from salary is one of the most important reasons take-home pay is lower than gross salary in Malaysia. The employee share reduces monthly salary received, while the employer share is paid separately by the employer.

How EPF affects salary

When employees look at a salary offer, EPF is usually the biggest regular deduction from gross salary. The deduction reduces monthly cash but contributes to retirement savings.

Employee share vs employer share

ShareWho pays?Does it reduce take-home pay?
Employee EPFEmployee, deducted through payrollYes
Employer EPFEmployerNo, it is separate from salary bank-in

Why official schedule matters

A simple 11% estimate is useful for planning, but official EPF contribution can involve schedules and rounding. For payroll, the official KWSP schedule should be used.

Example planning estimate

For a RM5,000 gross salary, a simple 11% employee EPF estimate is RM550. This means take-home pay before other deductions would already be lower than RM5,000.

When EPF rate may differ

EPF contribution can differ by employee status, age, nationality and applicable rules. Employees should check payslips and official KWSP information for the correct rate.

FAQ

Is EPF deducted from salary every month?

For eligible employees, the employee EPF share is deducted through payroll each month.

Does employer EPF come out of my salary?

No. Employer EPF is a separate contribution by the employer.

Is EPF always exactly 11%?

Not always. Employee category, age, nationality and official schedules can matter.

Why is my EPF amount not exactly 11%?

Official schedule and rounding can make the payroll amount differ from a simple percentage estimate.
